    About Hotel Jofa

    Hotel Jofa is located along the Migori–Sirare Highway in Migori County, Kenya. It serves local residents, business travellers, families, and travellers moving between Kenya and Tanzania.

    The hotel provides accommodation and hospitality services in a peaceful setting outside the busy town centre. It is suitable for accommodation, conferences, weddings, and family functions. The hotel opened in March 2020.
